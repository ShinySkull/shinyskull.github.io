Critter Crossing
Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 · Developer: ShinySkull Studio · App: Critter Crossing for Android

Critter Crossing is a sliding-puzzle game for players of all ages, including children. We built it to be simple about privacy: no accounts, no ads and no tracking. You can play the whole game offline. This policy explains in plain language what information is involved when you play and when a grown-up buys gems. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean ShinySkull Studio.

This policy lives at https://shinyskull.github.io/critter-crossing/privacy/.

The short version

  • No accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name, email address, phone number or photos.
  • No ads. The game has no advertising and no ad SDKs.
  • No analytics or tracking of our own. We don't run servers for the game and we don't receive information about how you play.
  • Your progress stays on your device. Stars, settings and your gem balance are saved in a file on your phone or tablet.
  • The shop is for grown-ups. Before the shop opens, the game asks for the player's age. Purchases and links that leave the game need a grown-up to pass a quick check.
  • Purchases go through Google Play. Google handles the payment. We never see card or bank details.
  • One exception to "nothing leaves your device": when a purchase is made, the purchase component we use (Unity In-App Purchasing, made by Unity Technologies) sends technical details about that purchase and the device to Unity. Details are in Buying gems.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

Information saved on your device

The game saves one file in its private storage on your device. Other apps can't read it. The file contains:

  • your progress: stars and best move counts for each level, Infinite Mode streaks, and which tutorials and tips you've already seen;
  • your settings: music and sound effects on or off, and their volume;
  • your gem balance, with totals of gems earned, bought and spent, plus a checksum that detects hand-edited saves;
  • which levels you've unlocked hints for;
  • the IDs of store transactions that have already been credited, so a purchase can never be credited twice;
  • the answer to the shop's age question (only whether the shop may be shown, not a birth date).

We don't receive this file. It stays on your device. If Android backup is turned on in your device settings, Android may include the file in your personal device backup to your Google Account. That backup is handled by Google under Google's terms, and we can't access it.

Deleting it: use Settings ▸ Reset progress in the game, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the game. Any of these deletes the save file from your device. Gems you've bought are stored in that file too, so resetting or uninstalling also removes them, unless Android later restores the file from your own device backup.

Buying gems (in-app purchases)

Gems are optional. Every level can be finished without buying anything, and gems are also earned by playing. You need an internet connection only to buy gems.

Age check and grown-up check. The first time someone opens the shop, the game asks for their age without suggesting an answer. If the player is under 13, the shop stays closed unless a grown-up passes a short check meant for adults. A grown-up check is also shown before every purchase and before any link that opens a web page outside the game. Only whether the shop may be shown is saved, on the device.

Google Play. Purchases are handled by Google Play's billing system. Google collects and processes payment information (such as a card or Google Play balance) under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. We never receive payment card or bank details. Google gives us basic order information so it can manage the sale, refunds and tax: what was bought, when, the price and an order number. Google Play's own family controls, such as purchase approval through Google Family Link and password protection for purchases, apply to every purchase.

On your device, the game uses the purchase receipt from Google Play to check the purchase, credit the gems once, and remember that the purchase was credited.

Unity In-App Purchasing. The game connects to Google Play through Unity In-App Purchasing, a software component made by Unity Technologies. Whenever a purchase is started, completed or cancelled, this component sends technical information about that purchase to Unity over an encrypted connection. Based on our review of the version we use, this includes:

  • purchase details: which gem pack, its price and currency, the Google Play order and purchase token, and whether the purchase succeeded, failed or was cancelled;
  • device and app details: device model, Android version, language and region settings, total storage size, when the device was last restarted, the app's version, and when it was installed.

It does not include a name, email address or payment details. We don't use a Unity account or Unity's online services, so this information isn't linked to a Unity player profile we control. As with any internet connection, Unity's servers also see the device's IP address.

Unity says it handles purchase transaction and billing records from this component as its own records (as a "data controller"), for running, securing and improving its purchasing service and for financial and legal record-keeping. See the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. We don't receive this information from Unity. We don't use it, or let it be used, to contact anyone, to build profiles or for advertising.

The component doesn't connect to Unity until a grown-up has been allowed into the shop. If the player said they're under 13 and no grown-up has unlocked the shop, nothing is sent to Unity.

Information we do not collect

We don't collect, receive or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, location, birth date, advertising ID or any other identifier that would let us identify you. The game doesn't ask for any special Android permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage access). It does have network access, which it needs for purchases.

How information is shared

  • We don't sell personal information, and we don't share it with advertisers or data brokers.
  • Information about purchases goes to Google (to process the payment) and to Unity Technologies (through the purchasing component described above). Each company handles it under its own privacy policy.
  • We may disclose information if the law requires it. Since we hold almost no information about players, there's usually nothing for us to disclose.

Children's privacy

Critter Crossing is made for everyone, including children. We designed it so that children can play without giving us any personal information:

  • There are no accounts, no chat, no sharing, no social features, no ads and no tracking. The game never asks a child for a name, email address, photo or location.
  • We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the age set by the law where you live).
  • The shop asks for the player's age without suggesting an answer. Players under 13 can't open the shop or buy anything unless a grown-up passes a check meant for adults. Until then, the game doesn't connect to Unity's purchasing service, so no purchase or device data is sent.
  • Every purchase also goes through Google Play, so parents and guardians can use Google Play's parental controls and Google Family Link to require approval for purchases or to block them entirely.
  • Links that open a web page outside the game (such as this policy) sit behind the grown-up check.
  • Gem packs are always shown with their real-money price, the game never pressures players to buy, and every level can be finished without spending money.

If a grown-up makes a purchase on a child's device, the purchase and device details described in Buying gems are sent to Unity, as explained there. Unity's servers also see the device's IP address. That information is used to operate, secure and keep records of the purchase service, not to contact anyone, build profiles or show ads.

Parents and guardians: we don't hold any information about your child on our side. To delete what the game stores on the device, use Settings ▸ Reset progress or uninstall the game. If you think your child has sent us personal information (for example, by emailing us), or you have any questions, contact us at trellistyouth@gmail.com and we'll delete it promptly. For purchase records held by Unity, you can contact Unity at DPO@unity3d.com. For Google Play order records, use Google's privacy tools for your Google Account.

Data security and retention

  • The save file stays in the app's private storage on your device until you reset progress, clear the app's data or uninstall the game.
  • Purchase-related data that Unity In-App Purchasing sends is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Unity and Google keep their records according to their own policies. Some records, such as transaction and billing records, may be kept for a period that tax and accounting laws require.
  • If you email us, we use your message only to answer you. We delete it when it's no longer needed to help you, normally within 12 months, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your choices and rights

  • You can play the whole game without an internet connection. If no purchase is made, the purchase-related data described above is never sent.
  • You can delete everything the game stores on your device at any time (see Information saved on your device).
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We don't hold player data ourselves. To exercise these rights over purchase data, contact Google (for payment and order data) or Unity at DPO@unity3d.com (for data sent by Unity In-App Purchasing). You can also email us at trellistyouth@gmail.com and we'll help where we can.
  • For refunds or questions about a charge, contact Google Play support. Consumer rights for digital purchases in your country still apply.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if we add new features. We'll change the effective date at the top and post the new version at this same address (https://shinyskull.github.io/critter-crossing/privacy/). If a change significantly affects how information is handled, especially information about children, we'll also mention it in the game or in the Google Play listing before it takes effect.
